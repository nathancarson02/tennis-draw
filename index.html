How this works
  • Tick the players who are here today, then hit Generate round.
  • The M / V pill on each player marks member or visitor. It's a label for you at the desk — it does not change the draw.
  • Bulk add takes a whole list at once — paste names one per line or separated by commas.
  • Select & edit turns the roster into a picker: click any tiles, then mark them member/visitor, toggle W/J, tick them in, or delete the lot in one go. Nothing pops up — deletes ask once, inline, and can be undone.
  • Courts fill 4 at a time (doubles). A leftover pair becomes a singles match. Odd numbers mean someone sits — the tool rotates who, never the same person twice in a row.
  • The W/J button marks women & juniors: the draw aims for one per side and never more than two on a court.
  • Mixups avoid repeating partners and foursomes from earlier rounds today.
  • Each round has two views — Courts and Table — switch with the buttons in the round header.
  • To fix a draw by hand, click one name, then another — they swap courts. Sat-out players can be swapped in too.
  • To sub in someone not on court at all, click the name coming off — a Replace box appears; pick the new player and hit Replace.
  • A singles court has a + Make doubles button — pick two more players and it becomes a doubles court.
  • The ◀ ▶ arrows under a court move that group to the neighbouring court — court numbers stay put, players shift.
  • Whole day → Timeline is the top-down view: every round stacked down the page, every court across. It's read-only on purpose — edit rounds in their own panel above.
  • Everything saves automatically in this browser on this computer. Print uses your browser's print box — choose "Save as PDF" there for a file.
